Mini Apartment in Forchtenstein

Located just above Forchtenstein bathing lake and close to Forchtenstein Castle, the apartment has its own parking space and a private entrance. The fully equipped kitchen includes all major appliances and a solid walnut worktop. The apartment also features a fresh-air ventilation system, an Atropa infrared cabin with deep-heat radiators available for a small additional charge, and a Tenne steam shower integrated into the living area. The bed is an Ada Trendline double sofa bed (160 cm) with an infrared panel. A large swivel TV, satellite TV, cable TV and Wi-Fi are provided. The little bathroom with toilet is separate. Guests also have exclusive use of a private fenced garden with beautiful seating areas.
