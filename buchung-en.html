📑 Booking Information

Check-in

Check-in is available from 3:00 p.m. If you expect to arrive later, please let us know.

Check-out

Please vacate the apartment by 12:00 noon on the day of departure.

Occupancy

The apartment accommodates a maximum of 2 guests.

Minimum Stay

The minimum stay is 2 nights.

Booking Deadline

Bookings can be made up to 24 hours before the planned arrival time.

Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available throughout your stay. The Wi-Fi password is provided in the apartment.

Parking

A free parking space is available after the S-bend, at the side of the paved turning area.

Wellness

Use of the Atropa infrared cabin is available for an additional charge of 11 euros per use (for up to 2 people).

Smoking

Smoking is permitted outdoors only.

Reservation and Deposit

A booking request is initially non-binding. The reservation becomes binding only after our personal confirmation.

No later than 14 days before arrival, a deposit of 50% of the agreed total price must be paid by bank transfer. The deposit will be fully credited towards the total price.

For bookings made within 14 days of arrival, the deposit is due immediately after personal booking confirmation.

You will receive the bank details and all further payment information with your personal booking confirmation.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation is free of charge up to 15 days before arrival.

For cancellations made from 14 days before arrival until the day before arrival, 50% of the agreed total price will be charged.

For cancellations on the day of arrival or in the event of a no-show, 100% of the agreed total price will be charged.

Any deposit already paid will be credited towards the applicable cancellation charges. Please notify us of a cancellation as early as possible in writing by email or message.

Tourism Tax

In accordance with the applicable regulations of the provincial government, a local tourism tax of currently 4.50 euros per person per night is payable for stays. The tax is collected by the host and remitted to the municipality.

Questions

If you have any questions before or during your stay, please feel free to contact us by phone or email.
