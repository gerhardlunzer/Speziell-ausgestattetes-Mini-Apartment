🏠 House Rules

Welcome

We are pleased to welcome you as our guest and wish you a pleasant stay.

Quiet Hours

Please be considerate of our neighbours between 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m.

Smoking

Smoking tobacco cigarettes, e-cigarettes and using vaporizers is permitted only in the covered and open outdoor areas of the apartment.

Pets

Pets are permitted only by prior arrangement.

Hanging Furniture in the Guest Garden

Hanging furniture is available for relaxation in our guest garden (maximum load: hammock 150 kg, hanging chair 100 kg). Please note that use is at your own risk, children must be supervised by an adult, and jumping into the hammock or hanging chair is not permitted.

Wi-Fi Use

Guests are responsible for any fines resulting from illegal downloads or violations of the law committed while using the guest Wi-Fi network.

Departure

Please leave the apartment tidy and inform us of any damage.
